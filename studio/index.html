📲 Want this studio as an app on your iPad? Tap for install steps
  1. Open this Studio page in Safari on your iPad (not inside a ZIP, Files, or another app's preview).
  2. Tap Safari's Share icon, choose Add to Home Screen, and tap Add.
  3. Open Upfront Art Studios from your Home Screen. Use your finger or Apple Pencil in Free Play.

Installation is free. Your art and lessons are saved locally on the iPad. In My Gallery, choose Export studio backup before changing devices or clearing browser data. Pencil pressure varies by device and Safari version.
