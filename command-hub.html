📲 Install the Hub and Art Studio on your iPad or phone
  1. On an iPad or iPhone, open this website directly in Safari. If you are inside ChatGPT, choose Open in Safari first.
  2. Tap the Share icon in Safari and choose Add to Home Screen. If needed, scroll the Share menu to find it.
  3. Tap Add. Open the new icon from your Home Screen. You can also install the Art Studio separately using the same steps.
